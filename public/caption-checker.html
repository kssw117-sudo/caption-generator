FAQ

How many hashtags can I use on Instagram now?Instagram now allows up to 5 hashtags per post and Reel. Pick a few specific, relevant ones instead of long lists.

Do hashtags count toward the character limit?Yes. On every platform, hashtags, spaces, line breaks and emoji all count as part of your caption.

Why is my count slightly different from the app?Some platforms count certain emoji and links differently. Treat this checker as a close estimate and leave a little room under the limit.
