FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to get started.

Does it actually look at my photo?

Yes. TagGenerator analyzes the photo you upload, or an extracted frame from your video, to write captions inspired by what is actually visible.

Can I create content without a photo?

Yes. Describe your topic or use batch mode to prepare multiple posts.

Is this a subscription?

No. The current license is a one-time $49 purchase. Daily generation limits apply, and extra credits are available.

Does it publish posts automatically?

No. You choose and copy your generated content, keeping control of what you publish.
